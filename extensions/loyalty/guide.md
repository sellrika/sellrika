# Loyalty Points

Customers earn points when they pay. At the goal they get a discount code.

## Set it up

1. Open **Settings › Extensions** and press **Configure** on Loyalty Points.
2. **How much must a customer pay to get 1 point?** With 1, an order of 25.90 gives 25 points. A smaller number gives points faster.
3. **How many points does a customer need for a reward?** This is the goal, for example 100.
4. **What does the discount code give?** Pick **Percent off** (10 = 10 %) or **Money off** (5 = 5.00 off) and type the size.
5. The right side shows an example with your numbers, like "About every 100.00 paid gives one 5.00 off code".
6. Leave the switch at the top on and press **Save**.

## How points are earned

- Only an order **with a customer** earns points. At the Sell screen, pick the customer before you take the money.
- Points are given when the order is **paid in full**. A Pay later order earns them when the last payment is recorded.
- Online orders count too, when the buyer is signed in to their store account.
- A **refund** takes back the points of the money refunded. A **void** takes back all points of that order.

## The reward

- When a customer reaches the goal, the goal is taken off their points and a discount code is made, like `LOYAL-7K2M9QXD`.
- The code works **once**. It is a normal code on the **Coupons** page: you can change or delete it there.
- In the online store, the buyer types the code at the checkout.

## How the customer hears about it

- **Email**: the code is emailed at once, when the customer has an email and the shop's email is set up.
- **Receipt**: every receipt of an order with a customer prints the points earned, their points, and each reward they have not used with its code.
- **At the Sell screen**: pick the customer and the cart shows their points. A reward they have not used shows as a button, **Use reward**. Press it and the discount is on the sale. The customer does not need to remember the code.
- **Online store**: a signed-in customer sees their points and their codes on **My account**.

## See the points

1. Open **Sales › Loyalty** in the sidebar.
2. The list shows each member, their points and the goal. Search by name or phone.
3. Press a member to see their history: the orders that gave points, points taken back, and each reward with its code.

## Good to know

- Changing the settings does not change points already given.
- A deleted customer loses their points and history.
- Points can't be changed by hand.
