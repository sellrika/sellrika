# Labels

Print barcode and price labels for the shop's products.

## Print labels

1. Open **Catalog › Labels** in the sidebar.
2. Search for a product and press **Add**. A product with variants shows one row per variant.
3. Set the copies with **−** and **+**, or type the number. Zero takes the product off the list.
4. The **Picked** tab shows only what you picked. **Clear all** empties the list.
5. On the right, check the label. The arrows walk through the picked products.
6. Pick the **Size**:
   - **50 × 30 mm** or **40 × 30 mm** for a label printer with a roll. One label per page.
   - **A4, 24 per sheet** for a normal printer with sticker paper (70 × 37 mm labels, 3 across, 8 down).
7. Under **Show**, switch the name, the price and the barcode on or off.
8. Press **Print N labels**. The browser's print window opens.

## Tips

- In the print window set **Margins** to none and **Scale** to 100, or the labels shift.
- For a roll, pick the paper size of the roll in the printer's settings.
- The barcode comes from the product's **Barcode** box (per variant when it has variants). A product with no barcode gets a label with no bars: fill in the Barcode on the product form first.
- The bars are Code 128. The Sell screen's scanner reads them like any other barcode.
- One print holds at most 2000 labels.
