# PromptPay QR

Take PromptPay payments with your own PromptPay ID. The customer screen shows a QR code for the exact amount. There is no provider and no fee.

The shop must sell in Thai baht (THB).

## Set it up

1. Open **Settings › Extensions**.
2. On **PromptPay QR**, press **Configure**.
3. Type your **PromptPay ID**: a phone number, a national ID or a tax ID. The money goes to this account.
4. You can also type the **Name under the QR code** and the **Account name**. They show under the QR code, so the customer can check it against the name in the bank app.
5. Save, then switch the extension on.
6. A payment method named **PromptPay** now shows on **Catalog › Payments**. You can rename it or change its icon and colour there.

## Take a payment

1. On the Sell screen, open the customer screen with the monitor button next to the search box.
2. Ring up the sale and press **Charge**.
3. Pick **PromptPay**. The customer screen shows a Thai QR Payment card with the QR code, the amount under it and the name from the settings.
4. The customer scans it with a bank app and pays.
5. **Check your bank app to see that the money arrived.** The app does not know. Then press the button to finish the sale.

It works the same way in **Record payment** on an order and in the Restaurant's **Pay** dialog.

## Tips

- Switching the extension off also switches the PromptPay method off. Past orders keep its name.
- No second monitor? The pay dialog shows the same QR code next to it. Turn your tablet or phone to the customer.
