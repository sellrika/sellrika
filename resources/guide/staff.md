# Staff

The Staff page holds the people who work in your shops. It has two tabs: **Staff** (people who sign in) and **Sales staff** (people who sell but do not sign in).
The page is in the sidebar under **Settings › Staff**. Managers and admins see it.

## The three roles

| Role | What they may do |
|---|---|
| **Cashier** | Sell at the till. See orders, print them, set the delivery status and record a payment. Add and change customers. Change their own settings. Switch shop. |
| **Manager** | Everything a cashier does. Also products, stock, categories, payment methods, marketing, reports, sales staff, and cashier accounts. |
| **Admin** | Everything. Also shops, the online store, coupons, extensions, Shop Email, MCP & API keys, refunds and voids, and making managers and admins. |

## How to add a staff member

1. Open **Settings › Staff**. Stay on the **Staff** tab.
2. Press **Add staff**.
3. Type the **Name** and the **Email**. They sign in with this email.
4. Look at the **Password** box. A random password is already there.
   - Press the first small button in the box (**Generate a new password**) to get another one.
   - Press the second small button (**Copy password**) to copy it.
5. Under **Role**, pick **Admin**, **Manager** or **Cashier**.
6. Press **Add staff**.
7. Give the email and the password to the person. The app does not send them.

## How to change a staff member

1. On the **Staff** tab, press **Edit** on their row.
2. Change the **Name**, the **Email** or the **Role**.
3. To give a new password, type it in **New password**. Leave it blank to keep the old one.
4. Press **Save**.

A new password signs that person out on all their devices.

## How to deactivate a staff member

1. On the **Staff** tab, find their row.
2. Switch **Active** off.

They are signed out at once and can not sign in any more. Their sales stay in the reports. Switch **Active** on to let them in again.

You can also press **Edit** and switch off **Active — can sign in**.

## How to delete a staff member

1. On the **Staff** tab, press the bin button on their row.
2. Press **Delete** to confirm.

Delete works only for a person with no sales history. If the bin button is grey, deactivate them instead.

## How to add a sales person

Sales staff belong to one shop. First switch to the right shop.

1. Open **Settings › Staff**.
2. Press the **Sales staff** tab.
3. Press **Add sales staff**.
4. Type the **Name**.
5. Type the **Commission** in percent, from 0 to 100. This is their share of each sale.
6. Press **Add sales staff**.

## How to name a sales person on a sale

1. On the **Sell** screen, add the items to the cart.
2. Press the **Charge** button in the cart. The checkout dialog opens.
3. Under **Sales staff (optional)**, pick the person. **No one** is the default.
4. Finish the sale as usual.

The **Sales staff (optional)** box shows only when the shop has at least one active sales person.

## How to change or switch off a sales person

1. On the **Sales staff** tab, press **Edit** on their row.
2. Change the **Name** or the **Commission**.
3. To switch them off, turn off **Active — can be picked at checkout**. You can also use the **Active** switch in the list.
4. Press **Save**.

A switched-off person is not offered at the checkout any more. Their old sales and their payment history stay.

## How to see what a sales person sold

1. On the **Sales staff** tab, press the person's name.
2. Pick the period at the top: **Today**, **Yesterday**, **7 days**, **Last week**, **This month**, **Last month**, **This year** or **Custom**. For **Custom**, set **From** and **To** and press **Apply**.
3. Read the four cards:
   - **Sold**: their sales in the period, after refunds.
   - **Commission**: their share of those sales, at today's rate.
   - **Sales**: how many orders. Voided orders are left out.
   - **Commission due**: what you have not paid them yet. This number does not follow the period.
4. Press **View orders** to see their orders.

## How to pay a commission

1. On the **Sales staff** tab, press **Pay** on their row. Or open the person and press **Pay** at the top.
2. Read the dialog. It shows the commission due, the rate, the amount sold and the number of sales since the last payment.
3. Press **Mark as paid**.

The payment is saved to the history. The commission due starts again from zero.
The app only records the payment. You hand over the money yourself.

## How to see the payment history

1. On the **Sales staff** tab, press the person's name.
2. Go down to **Payment history**.
3. Each row shows the date, the amount **Paid**, the number of **Orders**, the amount **Sold** and the **Commission** rate it was paid at.

The list on the **Sales staff** tab also shows **Due** and **Last paid** for each person.

## How to delete a sales person

1. On the **Sales staff** tab, press the bin button on their row.
2. Press **Delete** to confirm.

Their payment history is deleted. Their sales stay, but no longer show their name. This can not be undone. To keep the history, switch them off instead.

## Good to know

- Managers and admins can open the Staff page. Cashiers can not.
- A manager can only add, change and delete cashiers. A manager can only give the **Cashier** role. The other rows are grey for them.
- Staff belong to your account, not to one shop. A staff member can switch to every shop of the account.
- An email can be used for one account only. If it is taken, you see "That email is already in use".
- A password needs at least 8 characters.
- You can not deactivate, delete or lower the role of yourself.
- The shop owner always stays an active admin. Nobody can change that. Only the owner can change the owner's own account.
- A staff member with sales history can not be deleted. Deactivate them instead.
- Sales staff have no email and no password. They can never sign in.
- Sales staff are per shop. Switch shop to see the sales staff of another shop.
- The commission due is worked out each time at today's rate. If you change the rate, the amount due for all unpaid sales changes too.
- The commission due counts the sales made after the last payment. Voided orders do not count. Refunds are taken off.
- A refund on a sale that was already paid does not take the commission back.
- **Pay** is grey when there is nothing to pay.
