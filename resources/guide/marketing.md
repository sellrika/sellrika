# Marketing

The Marketing page sends email to your customers. It has two parts: the abandoned cart email and email campaigns.
The page is in the sidebar under **Sales › Marketing**. Managers and admins see it.

## What you need first

1. **Email must be set up.** If it is not, the page shows: "Outgoing email is not set up yet, so no marketing email goes out." An admin can set up the shop's own email under **Settings › Shop Email**.
2. **The online store must be on.** The emails link to it. If it is off, the page shows: "Switch on the online store first: the emails link to it."
3. **Customers need an email address.** A campaign goes only to customers of this shop who have one.

## How to switch on the abandoned cart email

A buyer starts the online checkout, types their email, and does not finish.
After some hours they get one email with their cart and a **Complete your order** button.

1. Open **Sales › Marketing**.
2. Press the gear button (**Settings**) at the top, next to **New campaign**.
3. Switch on **Send abandoned cart emails**.
4. Under **Send it after**, pick 1 hour, 4 hours, 12 hours or 24 hours.
5. Press **Save**.

To stop it, open the same dialog, switch it off and press **Save**.

## How to write a campaign

1. Open **Sales › Marketing**.
2. Press **New campaign**.
3. Type the **Subject**.
4. Write the **Message**.
5. Under **Products**, search and tick up to 6 products. This is optional. They show in the email as cards with picture, name, price and a link.
6. Under **Recipients**, tick the customers who get the email.
   - Use the search box to find customers by name or email.
   - **Select all** picks every customer that matches the search.
   - **Clear** unpicks everybody.
7. Press **Save campaign**.

The campaign is now a **Draft**. Nothing is sent yet.

## How to send a test to yourself

1. Open the saved campaign.
2. Press **Send test to me**.
3. The email goes to your own email address. Check how it looks.

## How to send a campaign now

1. Open the saved campaign.
2. If you changed something, press **Save campaign** first.
3. In the **Send** box, press **Send now**.
4. The emails start to go out within about a minute.

## How to schedule a campaign for later

1. Open the saved campaign.
2. In the **Send** box, pick the **Date** and the **Time**. They are in the shop's time zone.
3. Press **Schedule**.
4. The status changes to **Scheduled**.

The time must be in the future.

## How to cancel a schedule

1. Open the scheduled campaign.
2. Press **Cancel schedule**.
3. The campaign is a **Draft** again. You can change it and schedule it again.

## How to see what was sent

1. Open **Sales › Marketing**. The list shows each campaign with its **Status**, **Recipients**, **Sent / failed** and **Date**.
2. Press a sent campaign to open it.
3. You see the message, the number sent and failed, and each customer with **Sent** or the reason it was not sent.

The statuses are **Draft**, **Scheduled**, **Sending** and **Sent**.

## How to delete a campaign

1. Open the campaign.
2. Press the bin button at the top right.
3. Press **Delete campaign** to confirm.

Emails already sent stay sent.

## How unsubscribe works

- Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom.
- The customer opens the link and presses **Unsubscribe**.
- After that they get no more marketing emails from this shop. This covers campaigns and the abandoned cart email.
- Order emails still arrive. They are not marketing.
- In the **Recipients** list, such a customer shows **Unsubscribed** and can not be ticked.
- The customer list and the customer page show **Unsubscribed** too.

## Good to know

- Managers and admins can use the Marketing page. Cashiers can not.
- There is no newsletter sign-up form. You pick the customers by hand.
- A campaign can have up to 5000 recipients and up to 6 products.
- **Monthly limit**: an account can send 1000 campaign emails per calendar month through Sellrika's email. All shops of the account count together.
- When the limit is used up, **Schedule** and **Send now** are refused. The message says: "This account has used all its campaign emails for this month. Set up Shop Email to send more."
- A shop with its own **Shop Email** has no monthly limit.
- If the limit is reached while a campaign is sending, the rest of the emails fail with "Monthly limit reached".
- Only one campaign of the account sends at a time. If another one is still sending, **Send now** says: "Another campaign is still sending. Try again when it is done."
- **Schedule** and **Send now** need saved changes and at least one recipient.
- A campaign that is **Sending** or **Sent** can not be changed. A campaign can not be deleted while it is **Sending**.
- A sent email can not be taken back.
- The abandoned cart email goes out once per cart. It is not sent again if it fails.
- The abandoned cart email is not sent when the buyer has ordered since, has unsubscribed, or has started a newer checkout.
- An order with cash on delivery or bank transfer is made at once. So it never gets an abandoned cart email.
- The **Complete your order** link puts the items back in the buyer's cart and opens the checkout. Prices and stock are checked again there.
- The abandoned cart email is in the buyer's language. A campaign is in the store's main language.
