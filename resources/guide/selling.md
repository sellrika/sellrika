# Selling at the till

The **Sell** screen is where you make a sale: pick products, take the money, print a receipt. It is the first item in the sidebar, above the groups. Every role can use it.

The products are on the left. The **Cart** is on the right. On a phone, the cart is behind the **View cart** button at the bottom of the screen.

## How to find a product

1. Type a part of the product name in the search box at the top. The list updates as you type.
2. Or tap a category under the search box. Tap **All** to see every product again.
3. Scroll down to load more products.

Tip: press the `/` key to jump to the search box.

## How to show the cart to the customer on a second screen

You need a second monitor plugged into the same computer, facing the customer.

1. Tap the monitor button next to the search box.
2. In Chrome or Edge, the browser asks once if the site may manage windows on your displays. Choose **Allow**. The **Customer screen** then opens on the second monitor by itself. If you see "The browser blocked the window", tap the button again.
3. The customer screen goes full screen by itself with your next click on the Sell screen, for example when you tap a product. You can also click once on the customer screen.
4. In another browser, or if you chose not to allow it, drag the window to the second monitor yourself.
5. Sell as usual. The customer screen shows each item and the total as you add them.

When you take the money, the customer screen shows the picture of the payment method you pick, for example a QR code to pay. Add the picture on **Payments**: tap the pencil on the method, then **Upload picture**, then **Save**.

No second monitor? The pay dialog shows the same picture or QR code next to it, so you can turn your tablet or phone to the customer.

The customer screen works only in the same browser on the same computer. A tablet or another computer does not follow the cart.

## How to add a product with a barcode scanner

1. Scan the barcode. You do not need to click anywhere first.
2. The product goes into the cart.

You can also type the barcode in the search box and press Enter. If the barcode is unknown, you see "No product with barcode …".

## How to add a product to the cart

1. Tap the product. It goes into the cart with quantity 1.
2. Tap it again to add one more.
3. In the cart, use the **+** and **−** buttons to change the quantity.
4. Tap the trash icon on a line to remove it.

To empty the whole cart, tap **Clear**, then **Clear cart**. Tap **Keep** to cancel.

## How to sell a product with options (variants)

A product with options shows a badge like "3 options".

1. Tap the product. A window opens.
2. Choose one value in each group, for example a color and a size. A crossed-out value is not available or out of stock.
3. Set the quantity with **+** and **−**.
4. Tap **Add to cart**.

## How to sell a package

A package shows the **Package** badge.

1. Tap the package.
2. If the package has nothing to choose, it goes into the cart at once.
3. If it asks a question, choose one option for each part. The list **In this package** shows what is inside.
4. Set the quantity and tap **Add to cart**.

The window also tells you how many packages can still be built from the stock.

## How to change a line that is already in the cart

1. Tap the name or the picture of the line in the cart.
2. Change the options or the quantity.
3. Tap **Update line**. For a product with no options, tap **OK**.

## How to add a note to a cart line

A note says something about one line of the sale, like "no onions" or "gift wrap".

1. In the cart, press the speech-bubble button on the line.
2. Type the note and press Enter.
3. The note shows under the line. Press the button again to change it.

The note is saved with the order. It shows on the order page and prints on the receipt.

## How to attach a customer to the sale

1. In the cart, tap **Add customer**.
2. Type a name or a phone number. Tap the customer in the list.
3. If the customer is new, tap **New customer**. Fill in **Name** and, if you want, **Phone**. Tap **Add & attach**.

To pick another customer, tap **Change**. To remove the customer, tap the **X** next to the name. A customer is optional.

## How to give a discount

1. In the cart, tap **Discount** (above **Total**).
2. Choose percent (**%**) or a fixed amount (the currency sign).
3. Type the value and tap **Apply**. Or tap a quick button: **5%**, **10%**, **15%** or **20%**.

To take the discount off, open the same box and tap **Remove**.

## How to use a discount code

1. In the cart, tap **Discount**.
2. Type the code under **Discount code**.
3. Tap **Apply code**.

If the code does not work, you see the reason under the field. Some codes are only for certain customers. Attach the customer first, then apply the code.

## How to take the payment

1. Tap **Charge** at the bottom of the cart. The button shows the total, for example **Charge $18**.
2. Choose a **Payment method**.
3. For cash, type what the customer gives you in **Amount tendered**. Or tap **Exact**, or one of the quick amounts.
4. Check the **Change** line. This is the money you give back.
5. Tap **Confirm**. The button shows the total, for example **Confirm $18**.
6. You see "Sale complete". Tap **Print** for a receipt, then **Done**.

You can not confirm while the amount is less than the total.

## How to name the sales person

1. Open the payment window with **Charge**.
2. Choose a name in **Sales staff (optional)**. Leave it on **No one** if nobody should be named.

This list only shows when your shop has active sales staff.

## How to save the sale and take the money later (Pay later)

Use this for a customer who pays later, for example a table or a tab.

1. Fill the cart. Do not add a discount or a code.
2. Tap **Charge**.
3. Tap **Pay later**.
4. You see "Order saved, not paid yet" and the amount **Still due**. Tap **Print** if you need a slip, then **Done**.

The order is now on the **Orders** page as **Unpaid**. There you can add items, remove items and record the payment.

## How to print a receipt

1. After a sale, tap **Print** in the "Sale complete" window.
2. To print it again later, open the order on the **Orders** page and tap **Print**.

By default, the normal print window of your browser opens. To print straight to a thermal printer from an Android device:

1. Install the RawBT app on the device.
2. Open your user menu and choose **Settings**.
3. In **Printing**, switch on **Print straight to a thermal printer**.
4. Choose the **Paper width** that matches your printer: 58 mm or 80 mm.

This setting is saved on that device only.

## How the unfinished sale is kept

You do not need to do anything. If you open another page or reload, the cart is still there when you come back. The customer and the discount are kept too.

- The cart is kept for 1 day, on this device, for this shop.
- When it comes back, prices and stock are checked again. A product that is sold out or deleted is taken out of the cart.
- The cart is emptied after a sale, and when you tap **Clear**.

## Good to know

- Admins, managers and cashiers can all sell, give discounts and use **Pay later**.
- You can not add more of a product than there is in stock. A sold-out product shows **Out of stock**.
- A sale takes one discount only. A code replaces a discount you typed, and the other way round.
- If you change the cart and the code no longer fits, the code is removed and you get a message.
- Codes for free delivery do not work at the till.
- An unpaid order can not take a discount or a code. Remove it before you tap **Pay later**.
- **Pay later** takes the products out of stock at once. The order shows "Pay later" as its payment method.
- If your shop has a tax, the cart shows the tax line above **Total**.
- The payment methods come from the **Payments** page (sidebar: Catalog › Payments). Managers and admins can change them. Only a method that asks for the amount tendered shows the change.
- The server checks prices and stock again when you confirm. If something changed, you see a message and nothing is charged.
