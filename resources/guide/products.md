# Products and categories

The **Products** page holds everything your shop sells. The **Categories** page groups those products. Both are in the sidebar under **Catalog**.

## How to add a product

1. Open **Catalog › Products**.
2. Click **Add product**. The **New product** form opens on the **General** tab.
3. Type the **Name**. This is the only field you must fill.
4. In **Pricing**, type the **Base price**. This is what the customer pays.
5. Optional: fill **SKU**, **Barcode**, **Categories**, **Description**, **Images**, **Unit** and **Amount**.
6. Click **Save product**. You stay on the form, so you can keep editing.

To put a picture inside the **Description**, click the picture button in its toolbar, or paste or drop a picture.
Click the picture to change its width, to put it left, in the middle or right, to move it up or down, to describe it, to add a caption or to delete it.

## How to set the prices

All three prices are in the **Pricing** box of the **General** tab.

1. **Base price** is the selling price. New variants start with this price too.
2. **Purchase price** is your cost. Customers never see it. Reports use it to work out profit.
3. **Original price** is the old "was" price. The online store shows it crossed out next to the base price. It is never charged.

## How to edit or delete a product

1. Open **Catalog › Products**.
2. To find a product, use the search box (**Search by name, SKU or barcode**) or the **All categories** box.
3. To edit, click the product name or the pencil icon. Change what you need and click **Save product**.
4. To delete, click the red bin icon on the row, then click **Delete**.

A deleted product leaves your catalogue. Past orders keep their records.

## How to add pictures

1. On the product form, find the **Images** box.
2. Click **Add** and pick a photo, or drop a photo on the tile. To use a photo from a website, click **Link**, paste the `https://` link and click **Add link**.
3. The **Edit image** window opens. Every photo is saved as a square.
4. Drag the photo to move it. Use **Zoom** to make it bigger or smaller.
5. Optional: use **Padding**, **Background**, **Flip** and **Rotate**. **Undo** and **Redo** are at the top.
6. Click **Save** to keep the photo, or **Discard** to drop it.
7. Click **Save product**.

The first picture is the **Cover**. To change it, point at another picture and click the star (**Make cover**). To change a picture later, click it. To remove it, click the small **x** on it.

## How to remove the background of a photo

1. Open the photo in the **Edit image** window (add a new photo, or click one you already have).
2. Click **Remove background**. The first time it shows **Getting ready…** while it loads.
3. The product is cut out and placed in the middle. Pick a colour under **Background** if you want one.
4. Optional: move the **Shadow** slider to add a soft shadow. This slider works only after the background is removed.
5. Optional: click **Align center** to move the product back to the middle.
6. Click **Save**.

The work is done in your browser. The photo is not sent to another service.

## How to add variants (sizes, colours)

1. On the product form, open the **Variants** tab.
2. For a few variants, click **Add variant**. Type the name (for example "Small") and the price in the new row.
3. For many variants, click **Quick generate**. Type a group name (for example "Size") and its values with commas between them ("S, M, L"). Click **Add group** for a second group such as "Color". Then click the **Generate … variants** button. Every combination becomes one variant.
4. For each variant you can set **Price**, **Purchase price**, **SKU**, **Barcode** and **Images**. A variant with an empty price uses the base price.
5. To give all variants one price, type it in **Bulk set price** and click **Apply to all**.
6. Use the copy icon on a row to duplicate a variant, and the bin icon to remove it.
7. Click **Save product**.

The **Option groups** panel lists your groups and their values. Use **Add group** and the **Add value ↵** box to add more. Each variant row then picks one value for each group.

When a product has variants, the **SKU** and **Barcode** fields leave the **General** tab. You set them on each variant instead. The customer always buys one of the variants.

## How to make a package (bundle)

A package is several products sold together at one price, for example "Pen, 1 dozen".

1. On the product form, switch on **This is a package of other products**.
2. Set the **Base price** of the package.
3. Open the **Contents** tab. (The **Variants** tab changes its name to **Contents**.)
4. Find a product in the search box and click it to add it.
5. Type how many of that product are in the package.
6. If the product has variants, click the variants that are allowed. With two or more allowed, the row shows **Customer picks one** and the choice is made at the sale.
7. Read the line under the list. It shows **Contents value**, **Package price** and how much the customer saves.
8. Click **Save product**.

A package needs at least one product inside. It has no purchase price, no variants and no stock of its own. A package can not contain another package.

## How to set the barcode, SKU and unit

1. **SKU** is your own product code. It is optional. You can search by it.
2. **Barcode** is optional. Scan it or type it. Scanning it on the Sell screen adds the product to the cart.
3. **Unit** is how the product is counted (pcs, kg, m…). Pick one from the list or type your own.
4. **Amount** is how much one unit holds, for example 50 with the unit "g" for a 50 g pack.

## How to feature or hide a product

1. To feature a product, click the star on its row in the list. Or switch on **Featured** on the form. Featured products are shown first on the Sell screen, the online store and the product list.
2. To hide a product, switch off **Active** on its row or on the form. It leaves the Sell screen and the online store. It stays on the product list in grey. Switch it on again any time.

## How to add additional data

1. On the product form, open the **Additional data** tab.
2. Click **Add row**.
3. Type a **Title** (for example "Brand") and a **Value**.
4. Click **Save product**.

These rows are shown above the description on the Sell screen and in the online store.

## How to ask the buyer for a note (custom request)

1. On the **General** tab, switch on **Custom request**.
2. Type the question in **Question to the buyer**, for example "Name to engrave".
3. Click **Save product**.

The online store then asks every buyer of this product to write a note. The note is saved with the order line as **Note**. The Sell screen never asks for it, but staff can add their own note to any cart line there (the speech-bubble button).

## How to manage categories

1. Open **Catalog › Categories**.
2. To add one, click **Add category**. Type a name, pick a colour and a picture if you want, and click **Add**.
3. To add a picture, click **Add** under **Picture** and pick a file, or click **Link** and paste an `https://` link. The image editor opens, the same one as for a product picture. Change the picture there and click **Save**.
4. To change a category's name, colour or picture, click its pencil icon. Change it in the dialog and click **Save**.
5. To delete one, click its bin icon, then **Delete**. Its products stay. They only lose this category.
6. To put a product in categories, open the product and click the category names under **Categories**. A product can be in many categories.
7. To add categories to many products at once, tick the products on the **Products** list, click **Add to categories**, tick the categories and click **Add**. The products keep the categories they already have.

## How to export and import products (CSV)

1. Open **Catalog › Products**.
2. Click **Export** to download all products as a CSV file. A product with variants has one row for each variant. Packages are not in the file.
3. Click **Import** and choose a CSV file to add products.
4. A message shows how many products were imported. Rows with a problem are skipped and listed in a red box. Click **Dismiss** to close it.

The first row of the file must be a header with at least the columns `name` and `price`. The best way to get the right format is to export first and use that file as a model.

## How to share a product

1. The online store must be on, and the product must be **Active**.
2. On the **Products** list, click the share icon on the row. Or open the product and click **Share**.
3. Click **Copy** to copy the **Product link**. Or click Facebook, X, WhatsApp, Telegram, LINE or Viber to post it there.
4. On a phone, **More apps…** opens the phone's own share menu.

## Good to know

- Admins and managers can use the Products and Categories pages. Cashiers can not.
- A shop can have up to 500 products. After that, a new product is refused.
- A product can have up to 10 pictures. Allowed files: JPG, PNG, WEBP, AVIF or GIF, up to 5MB each.
- A barcode can be used only once in a shop, across all products and variants.
- Import only adds new products. It never changes the products you already have.
- An import file can be up to 2 MB and 5000 rows. If the file's currency is not the shop's currency, nothing is imported.
- You can not delete a product that is inside a package. Take it out of the package first. The same is true for a variant that a package uses.
- A package is not sold while one of the products inside it is switched off. The list shows **Not sold: a product inside is switched off**.
- Deleting a product can not be undone. Its stock history is deleted too. A picture you remove from a product is deleted as well.
- The product form does not change stock counts. Use **Catalog › Stock** for that.
- Prices are shown like $18 or $18.23.
