# Sellrika overview

Sellrika is a point of sale and an online store in one app.
You sell at the till on the **Sell** screen. Buyers order from your online store.
Both use the same products, the same stock and the same orders.

This guide has one topic per area of the app. Read the topic that fits the question.

## The topics

- `selling` — the **Sell** screen: build a cart, take a payment, **Pay later**, receipts.
- `orders` — the **Orders** page: find an order, record a payment, refund, void, delivery steps, add or remove items.
- `customers` — the **Customers** page: add, edit and delete buyers, see their history.
- `coupons` — the **Coupons** page: discount codes for the online checkout and the till.
- `marketing` — the **Marketing** page: the abandoned-cart email and email campaigns.
- `reports` — the **Reports** page: sales, profit and tax for a period.
- `products` — the **Products** and **Categories** pages: prices, pictures, variants, packages, CSV import.
- `stock` — the **Stock** page: counts, receiving stock, corrections, the history.
- `online-store` — the **Online Store** page: switch the store on, its address, the template, languages, currencies, alerts.
- `store-checkout` — the **Online Store** page: how buyers pay, delivery fees, pickup, buyer accounts.
- `staff` — the **Staff** page: staff accounts, roles, sales staff and commission.
- `settings` — shops, tax, the **Payments** page, **Shop Email**, **Extensions**, **MCP & API keys**, your profile.

## The menu

The sidebar on the left has these groups.

- **Sell** — the till. It is the first page after you sign in.
- **Sales** — **Reports**, **Orders**, **Customers**, **Coupons**, **Marketing**.
- **Catalog** — **Products**, **Stock**, **Categories**, **Payments** (the payment methods of the till).
- **Settings** — **Online Store**, **Staff**, **Extensions**, **Shop Email**, **MCP & API keys**.

The **Search** box at the top of the sidebar finds pages, orders, customers and products.
The shop name at the top of the sidebar opens the list of your shops. Pick one to work in it.
Your name at the bottom opens the user menu: your profile, the language, and **Log out**.

## Shops

One account can have several shops.
Each shop has its own products, stock, orders, customers, payment methods and online store.
Everything you see belongs to the shop picked in the sidebar.

## Roles

Every staff account has one role. The menu shows only what the role may use.

- **Cashier** — sells, reads orders, works with customers, changes their own profile.
- **Manager** — everything a cashier does, plus products, stock, categories, payment methods, marketing, reports and cashier accounts.
- **Admin** — everything in their shops, also the online store, coupons, extensions, email and API keys.

## Good to know

- Money is shown without cents when the amount is whole, like $18. Otherwise it is shown like $18.23.
- Times and "today" follow the time zone of the shop.
- An order is never deleted. A wrong order is voided or refunded.
- A stock count is never typed over. Every change is a line in the stock history.
- The app speaks English, German and Thai. Change it in the user menu.
- This guide explains the screens. An assistant with an API key can also do many of these tasks with its other tools.
