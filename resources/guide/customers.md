# Customers

The **Customers** page keeps the people who buy from your shop, and what each one bought. Open it in the sidebar: Sales › **Customers**. Every role can use it.

The list shows each customer with their email, the number of **Orders**, the **Total spent** and the date of the **Last order**.

## How to add a customer

1. Click **Add customer** at the top right.
2. Type the **Name**.
3. If you want, fill in **Phone**, **Email** and **Note**.
4. Click **Add customer**.

Only the name is required.

## How to add an address to a customer

1. Open the customer from the list.
2. In the **Addresses** card, click **Add address**.
3. Type the **Address**. The label, phone, city, state, postal code, country and **Tax ID** are optional. The tax ID is for invoices.
4. Click **Save**.

A customer can have several addresses. Use the pencil to change one and the bin to remove it. A customer who signs in to your online store sees the same addresses on their account.

## How to add a customer during a sale

1. On the **Sell** screen, click **Add customer** in the cart.
2. Click **New customer**.
3. Type the **Name** and, if you want, the **Phone**.
4. Click **Add & attach**.

The customer is saved and attached to the sale in one step.

## How to find a customer

1. Type in the search box at the top of the page.
2. You can search by name, phone or email. The list updates as you type.

## How to sort the list

1. Click a column title: **Customer**, **Email**, **Orders**, **Total spent** or **Last order**.
2. Click the same title again to sort the other way.
3. Click it a third time to go back to the normal order, newest customers first.

## How to see what a customer bought

1. Click the customer in the list.
2. At the top you see the phone, the email and the date they became a customer.
3. The three boxes show **Orders**, **Total spent** and **Last order**.
4. **Note** shows what you wrote about this customer.
5. **Purchase history** lists their orders. Click an order to open it.

To see the same orders on the **Orders** page, with all its filters, click **View in Orders**.

## How to edit a customer

1. Open the customer.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Change the **Name**, **Phone**, **Email** or **Note**.
4. Click **Save**.

## How to delete a customer

1. Open the customer.
2. Click the trash icon at the top right.
3. Read the question and click **Delete**. Click **Cancel** to keep the customer.

The customer's past orders are kept. They are only no longer linked to a customer. On the order page, the **Customer** box then says "Walk-in (no customer attached)". This can not be undone.

## Good to know

- Admins, managers and cashiers can add, edit and delete customers.
- Customers belong to one shop. Another shop of your account has its own list.
- A phone number can be used by one customer only in a shop. If it is taken, you see "Phone already used by …" with the name of the other customer.
- **Orders**, **Total spent** and **Last order** do not count voided orders. **Total spent** is what the customer paid less what was refunded.
- Deleting a customer does not delete or change the money of any order. Sales totals and reports stay the same.
- A deleted customer can not be brought back. You can add the person again, but the old orders stay without a customer.
- People who order from your online store are added to this list too.
- The **Unsubscribed** badge means this person no longer wants marketing emails from your shop.
